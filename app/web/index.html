About the model and its limits

Model. MobileNetV2 (ImageNet weights) with the whole backbone fine-tuned on PlantVillage, 38 classes. On the official test split (10,709 lab photos, scored once): macro-F1 0.982, accuracy 98.6%, top-3 accuracy 99.96%. The split keeps every leaf on one side, so photos of a test leaf were never seen in training.

"Not sure". If the top class has a probability below 94.7% (a threshold chosen on the validation split), no diagnosis is given. On the test split this happens for 3.2% of the photos; the answered ones are 99.5% correct.

Heat map (Grad-CAM). Red areas raised the score of the top class the most. The map is coarse (about 1/7 of the photo per cell): it shows a region, not single spots.

Limits. The training photos are single leaves on plain lab backgrounds. Field photos (several leaves, soil, strong shadows) are harder and were not tested. Crops and diseases outside the 38 classes are not recognised as unknown: they get the closest known class, sometimes with high confidence.

How it runs. The model (9 MB, downloaded once) is executed by ONNX Runtime Web on your device. A photo is cut to its centred square and resized exactly as in training; the result matches the Python model of release v1.0. Code, data checks and the full evaluation: GitHub.

⚠️ Educational prototype. It gives preliminary information only and does not replace an agronomist's diagnosis. Example photos: PlantVillage (Hughes & Salathé, 2015), CC BY-SA 3.0.
